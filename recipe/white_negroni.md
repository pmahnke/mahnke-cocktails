### Ingredients

|  Amount | Ingredient   | Brand        |
| ------: | ------------ | ------------ |
|  1.5 oz | Plymouth Gin | Plymouth     |
| 0.75 oz | Amaro        | Suze         |
| 0.75 oz | Lillet Blanc | Lillet Blanc |

### Notes

- Method: Stirred
- Pour: Julep Strain
- Glassware:
  - Low Ball
  - Large Ice cube
- Garnish: Grapefruit twist
- Special Prep: Express grapefruit oil on the drink.

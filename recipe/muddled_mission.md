  Created in 2008 by bartender Joaquín Simó during his time at New York City’s pioneering Death & Co., the Muddled Mission is a brilliant exercise in unexpected flavor pairings. The cocktail's name plays like a cryptic crossword clue, offering a subtle nod to the Carthusian monks—men on a holy mission—who distill the Chartreuse that gives this drink its complex, herbaceous backbone. While alpine spirits are rarely paired with muddled fresh fruit, Simó struck gold by bridging the gap between bright summer produce and heavy monastic liqueurs, resulting in a contemporary classic that remains a staple of the modern cocktail renaissance.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Brand |
| -----: | ---------- | ----- |
| 1.5 oz | London Dry Gin | Hayman's London Dry |
| 1 oz | Elderflower Liqueur | St-Germain |
| 0.25 oz | Yellow Chartreuse | Yellow Chartreuse |
| 0.75 oz | Fresh Lemon Juice |  |
| 1 | Strawberry | |

## Notes

- Method: Shaken
- Pour: Double Strain
- Glassware: 
  - Coupe glass
- Garnish: strawberry
- Special Prep:
  - Muddle the strawberry in the base of a shaker.
  - Add other ingredients and shake with ice.
  - Strain into a chilled glass.

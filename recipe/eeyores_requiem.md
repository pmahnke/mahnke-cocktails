excerpt: |
  Created around 2009 by legendary mixologist Toby Maloney at The Violet Hour in Chicago, Eeyore’s Requiem is a careful deconstruction of the classic Negroni. Rather than using gin as the primary spirit, Maloney dramatically inverted the traditional template by using Campari as the dominant base.
  
  He dubbed the concoction after A.A. Milne’s famously gloomy donkey, Eeyore—affectionately citing him as "the most bitter character in literature". Gin acts in a supporting role to anchor the botanical structure, while aromatic orange bitters and expressed citrus oils brighten each sip. The result is a brooding, multi-layered aperitif that begins with sharp citrus and finishes with a lingering, herbaceous alpine bite.

## The Original

| Amount | Ingredient | Brand |
| -----: | ---------- | ----- |
| 1.5 oz | Campari |  |
| 1 oz | Blanc Vermouth | Dolin Blanc Vermouth de Chambéry |
| 1/2 oz | London Dry Gin | The Botanist Islay Dry Gin |
| 1/4 oz | Cynar |  |
| 1/4 oz | Fernet Branca |  |
| 1.5 dashes | 50/50 bitters | Half Regan’s Orange Bitters No. 6 and half Fee Brothers Orange Bitters |
| 3 swaths | Orange Peel |  |


## Notes

- Method: Stirred
- Pour: Julep Strain
- Glassware: 
  - Small Low Ball glass
- Garnish: orange twist
- Special Prep: Squeeze three swaths of orange peel across the top of the glass

## Rebalanced (per Anders Ericson)

| Amount | Ingredient | Brand |
| -----: | ---------- | ----- |

| 2 dashes | 50/50 bitters | Half Regan’s Orange Bitters No. 6 and half Fee Brothers Orange Bitters |
| 1 swath | Orange Peel |  |

## Notes

- Method: Stirred
- Pour: Julep Strain
- Glassware: 
  - Small Low Ball glass
- Garnish: orange peel, orange twist
- Special Prep: 
  - Squeeze one swaths of orange peel across the top of the glass
  - Served on a large ice cube

  Created in the 1990s by bartender Emmanuel Audermatte, the DNA No. 1 is a modern, easy-going variation on the classic Gin Sour. By utilizing apricot liqueur as a primary sweetening agent alongside a touch of sugar syrup, the drink takes on a rich, stone-fruit complexity that perfectly complements the botanical notes of London Dry Gin. It functions as a bright, "genteel sipper"—slightly tart, very fruity, and beautifully rounded by the orange bitters and citrus twist.

### Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Brand |
| -----: | ---------- | ----- |
| 2 oz | London Dry Gin | Botanist Islay Dry Gin  |
| 0.75 oz | Apricot Liqueur | 	Giffard Abricot du Roussillon|
| 0.75 oz | Freshly squeezed lemon juice |  |
| 0.25 oz | [Semi-rich Simple Syrup]({%link recipe/simple_syrup.md%}) |  |
| 2 dashes | Orange bitters | Peychaud’s Orange Bitters |

### Notes

- Method: Shaken
- Pour: Double Strain
- Glassware: 
  - Martini glass
  - None
- Garnish: Orange twist
- Special Prep: Shake all ingredients with ice and fine strain into chilled glass. Garnish with orange twist.

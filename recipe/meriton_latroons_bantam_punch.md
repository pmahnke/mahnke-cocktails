## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient       | Brand                     |
| -----: | ---------------- | ------------------------- |
|    6 g | Ambergris        |                           |
|   6 oz | Gula Jawa Sugar  |                           |
|   6 oz | Fresh Lime Juice |                           |
|  25 oz | Batavia Arrack   | Batavia Arrack van Oosten |
|  32 oz | Water            |                           |

## Notes

- Method: Build in Punch Bowl
- Pour: Add all ingredients directly to the punch bowl
- Glassware:
  - Punch Cups
- Garnish: Nutmeg
- Special Prep:
  - Add the ambergris and one ounce of the gula jawa sugar to a mortar and grind to combine the ingredients into a fine powder.
  - Add two ounces of arrack to the mortar and stir to dissolve the mixture. Then add this to the punch bowl.
  - Add the lime juice and the remaining five ounces of gula jawa to the punch bowl and stir to dissolve the sugar.
  - Add the the remaining 23 ounces of arrack and the water, and stir to combine.
  - Garnish with grated nutmeg.

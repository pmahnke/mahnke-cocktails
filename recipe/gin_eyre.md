### Ingredients

|        Amount | Ingredient                                                    | Brand     |
| ------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
|           2oz | London Dry Gin                                                |
|        0.5 oz | Triple Sec                                                    | Cointreau |
|        0.5 oz | [Semi-Rich Simple Syrup]({%- link recipe/simple_syrup.md -%}) |
|          1 oz | Fresh Lemon Juice                                             |
|      2 dashes | Orange Bitters                                                |
| 6 to 8 Leaves | Mint                                                          |

### Notes

- Method: Shaken
- Pour: Double Strain
- Glassware: Coupe
- Garnish: Mint leaf
- Special Prep: Expressed orange oil

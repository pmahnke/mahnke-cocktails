## Ingredients

|   Amount | Ingredient          | Brand                  |
| -------: | ------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 2 dashes | Bitters             | Peychaud's             |
|     1 oz | Fresh Lime Juice    |                        |
|   0.5 oz | Passion Fruit Syrup |                        |
|     1 oz | White Rum           | Banks                  |
|  0.75 oz | Jamaican rum        | Appleton Estate 8 Year |
|   0.5 oz | Dark Rum            | Worthy Park 109        |

## Notes

- Method: Blended
- Pour: Open Pour
- Glassware:
  - High Ball glass
  - Crushed Ice
- Garnish: pineapple slice, mint

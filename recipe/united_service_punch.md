## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient        | Brand                     |
| -----: | ----------------- | ------------------------- |
|  12 oz | Oleo-Saccharum    |                           |
|  40 oz | Black Tea         |
|   8 oz | Fresh Lemon Juice |                           |
|  20 oz | Batavia Arrack    | Batavia Arrack van Oosten |

## Notes

- Method: Build in Punch Bowl
- Pour: Add all ingredients directly to the punch bowl
- Glassware:
  - Punch Cups
- Garnish: None
- Special Prep:
  - Add the lemon peels of 4 lemons and 12 oz of demerara sugar to a zip top bag and massage the sugar into the peels. Let this sit for 24 hours.
  - Add the oleo-saccharum (peals and all) to the punch bowl.
  - Add the black tea, arrack and the lemon juice to the punch bowl.
  - Add additional lemon juice to taste.
  - Serve either hot or cold.

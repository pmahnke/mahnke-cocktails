## Ingredients

|        Amount | Ingredient                                          | Brand      |
| ------------: | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
|          2 oz | London Dry Gin                                      | Beefeaters |
|       0.75 oz | [Cinnamon Syrup]({%link recipe/cinnamon_syrup.md%}) |  
|       0.75 oz | Fresh Lemon Juice                                   |  
| 6 to 8 leaves | Basil                                               |  

## Notes

- Method: Shaken
- Pour: Double Strain
- Glassware: 
  - Coupe glass
- Garnish: Basil
- Special Prep: Muddle the basil leaves with the cinnamon syrup in the cheater tin

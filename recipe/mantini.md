## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Brand |
| -----: | ---------- | ----- |
| 1 oz | Rye | Rittenhouse 100 Proof Rye Whiskey |
| 1 oz | London Dry Gin | Martin Miller’s Gin |
| 0.5 oz | Sweet Vermouth | Cocchi Vermouth di Torino |
| 0.5 oz | Dry Vermouth | Dolin Vermouth de Chambéry Dry |
| 1 dash | Bitters | Angostura Bitters |
| 1 dash | Orange Bitters | Regan’s Orange Bitters No. 6 |
| 2 dashes | Absinthe | St. George Absinthe Verte (optional) |

## Notes

- Method: Stirred
- Pour: Julep Strain
- Glassware: 
  - Martini glass
- Garnish: cherry and lemon peel
- Special Prep: Express lemon oil

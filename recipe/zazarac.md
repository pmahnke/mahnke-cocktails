  The Zazarac emerged in the late 19th century at the Sazerac House in New Orleans, where it originally evolved alongside the classic Sazerac as a sweeter, rum-and-anisette-spiked variant. It gained international fame when Harry Craddock preserved its unique proportions in London's iconic 1930 Savoy Cocktail Book, saving it from being entirely forgotten in favor of its rye-forward cousin.

### Ingredients

|   Amount | Ingredient                                                | Brand                      |
| -------: | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
|     1 oz | Rye                                                       | Canadian Club              |
|   0.5 oz | White Rum                                                 | Bacardi                    |
|   0.5 oz | Anisette                                                  |
|   0.5 oz | [Semi-rich Simple Syrup]({%link recipe/simple_syrup.md%}) |
|   1 dash | Bitters                                                   | Angostura Aromatic Bitters |
|   1 dash | Orange Bitters                                            |
| 3 dashes | Absinthe                                                  |

### Notes

- Method: Shaken
- Pour: Double Strain
- Glassware: Nick & Nora
- Garnish: None
- Special Prep: Express lemon oil into cocktail

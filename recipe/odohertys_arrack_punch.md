## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient       | Brand                     |
| -----: | ---------------- | ------------------------- |
|   2 oz | Demerara Sugar   |                           |
|   2 oz | Water, Hot       |
|   2 oz | Fresh Lime Juice |                           |
|   6 oz | Batavia Arrack   | Batavia Arrack van Oosten |
|   4 oz | Jamaican Rum     | Smith & Cross             |
|  12 oz | Water            |                           |

## Notes

- Method: Build in Punch Bowl
- Pour: Add all ingredients directly to the punch bowl
- Glassware:
  - Punch Cups
- Garnish: Nutmeg
- Special Prep:
  - Add the sugar, lime juice and hot water to the bowl and stir to combine.
  - Add the the arrack, rum and the water, and stir to combine.
  - Refrigerate to chill.
  - Garnish with grated nutmeg.

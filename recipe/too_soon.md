  Created by legendary bartender Sam Ross at New York's renowned Attaboy, the Too Soon stands proudly alongside his other modern classics like the Penicillin and the Paper Plane. While those widely known drinks lean heavily on whiskey, this inventive recipe takes a decidedly different path by building an amaro-laced gin sour that challenges and rewards the palate.
  
  The cocktail relies on a striking core of dry gin, fresh lemon juice, and simple syrup, anchored by a hefty pour of Cynar—a bold, vegetal Italian artichoke amaro. What truly sets the drink apart, however, is the addition of two fresh orange slices tossed directly into the shaker. As the drink is agitated, the sweet, bright oils from the orange peel effortlessly mellow the Cynar's bitter edge, resulting in a beautifully balanced, bittersweet aperitif with a complex citrus finish.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Brand |
| -----: | ---------- | ----- |
| 1 oz | London Dry Gin | Beefeater |
| 1 oz | Cynar | Cynar |
| 0.75 oz | Freshly squeezed lemon juice |  |
| 0.5 oz | [Semi-rich Simple Syrup]({%link recipe/simple_syrup.md%}) |  |
| 2 Slices | Orange |  |

## Notes

- Method: Shaken
- Pour: Double Strain
- Glassware: 
  - Coupe glass
- Garnish: None
- Special Prep:
  - Add two orange slices to your cocktail shaker
  - Measure the remaining ingredients into your shaker
  - Shake with ice
  - Double strain into a chilled coupe glass

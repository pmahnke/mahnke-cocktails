## Ingredients

|  Amount | Ingredient     | Brand                     |
| ------: | -------------- | ------------------------- |
|   16 oz | Demerara Sugar |                           |
|   32 oz | Water, Hot     |
| 6 whole | Lemons         |                           |
|   25 oz | Batavia Arrack | Batavia Arrack van Oosten |

## Notes

- Method: Build in a Jug
- Pour: Add all ingredients directly to the Jug
- Glassware:
  - Punch Cups
- Garnish: Nutmeg
- Special Prep:
  - Slice the six lemons into thin wheels, and add to a storage container
  - Add the arrack to the storage container with the lemons and soak for at least 6 hours.
  - Remove the lemon wheels and try not to crush them.
  - Add the sugar and hot water to a jug and stir to combine.
  - Add the the lemon infused arrack, and stir to combine.
  - Refrigerate to chill.
  - Garnish with grated nutmeg.

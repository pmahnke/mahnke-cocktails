  The Rapscallion was originally created by Craig Harper in 2003 at The Hallion. A private club at the time in Edinburgh. Adeline Shepherd took the cocktail to her bar, Ruby in Copenhagen in 2005. She switched the scotch from Johnnie Walker Black to Talisker.

  It’s a deceptively simple but nicely potent cocktail, subtly sweet and smoky. It’s a variation on the Manhattan, and more specifically the Rob Roy, which is a Manhattan made with Scotch.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Brand |
| -----: | ---------- | ----- |
| 1.5 oz | Single Malt Scotch | Talisker 10 Year |
| 0.75 oz | Pedro Ximenez Sherry | Lustau San Emilio |
| 1 rinse | Pastis | Ricard Pastis de Marseille Aperitif |

## Notes

- Method: Stirred
- Pour: Julep Strain
- Glassware: 
  - Coupe glass
- Garnish: Lemon peel
- Special Prep: Expressed lemon oil

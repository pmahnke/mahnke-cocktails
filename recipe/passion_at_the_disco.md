### Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient                                                    | Brand      |
| -----: | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
|   1 oz | Fresh Lime Juice                                              |
|   1 oz | [Passion Fruit Syrup]({%link recipe/passion_fruit_syrup.md%}) |
|   2 oz | Indian Tonic Water                                            | Fever Tree |

### Notes

- Method: Shaken
- Pour: Double Strain
- Glassware: Low Ball
- Garnish: Mint leaf
- Ice: Ice cubes
- Special Prep: 
  - Add lime juice and passion fruit syrup to the shaker with ice and shake. 
  - Pour the mocktail into the glass and top with tonic water.

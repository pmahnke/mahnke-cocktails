  Bombay Presidency Punch was first documented in an official letter written on October 15, 1694, by Sir John Gayer, the Governor of the Bombay Presidency.
  Gayer wrote to his subordinates at the factory in Surat, chiding them for their excessive lifestyle but providing a precise recipe for what he considered a proper, orderly punch bowl. His recipe utilized Goa arrack (a spirit distilled from toddy palm sap or cashew wood), lime juice, jaggery (unrefined palm sugar), water, and nutmeg.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient             | Brand |
| -----: | ---------------------- | ----- |
|   8 oz | Jaggery Sugar          |       |
|   8 oz | Fresh Lime Juice       |       |
|  32 oz | Sri Lankan Palm Arrack |       |
|  40 oz | Water                  |       |

## Notes

- Method: Build in Punch Bowl
- Pour: Add all ingredients directly to the punch bowl
- Glassware:
  - Punch Cups
- Garnish: Nutmeg
- Special Prep:
  - Add the jaggery and lime juice to the bowl and muddle to dissolve the sugar
  - Add the arrack to the bowl, and stir to combine
  - Add the water, and stir to combine
  - Garnish with grated nutmeg

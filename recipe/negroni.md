### Ingredients

|  Amount | Ingredient     | Brand                     |
| ------: | -------------- | ------------------------- |
|  1.5 oz | London Dry Gin | The Botanist              |
| 0.75 oz | Amaro          | Campari                   |
| 0.75 oz | Sweet Vermouth | Cocchi Vermouth di Torino |

### Notes

- Method: Stirred
- Pour: Julep Strain
- Glassware:
  - Low Ball
  - Large Ice cube
- Garnish: None
- Special Prep: Flamed Orange Rind

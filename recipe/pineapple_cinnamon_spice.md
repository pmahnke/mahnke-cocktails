## Ingredients

|   Amount | Ingredient                                          | Brand                      |
| -------: | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
|     2 oz | Jamaican Rum                                        | Appleton Estate 8 Year     |
|  0.75 oz | Amaro                                               | Averna                     |
|   0.5 oz | [Cinnamon Syrup]({%link recipe/cinnamon_syrup.md%}) |                            |
|     1 oz | Fresh Pineapple Juice                               |                            |
|  0.75 oz | Fresh Lime Juice                                    |                            |
| 2 dashes | Bitters                                             | Angostura Aromatic Bitters |

## Notes

- Method: Shaken
- Pour: Open Pour
- Glassware:
  - Low Ball glass
  - Crushed Ice
- Garnish: pineapple slice, mint sprig, tropical flower, cinnamon stick
- Special Prep: 
  - Shake the cocktail and pour into the glass
  - Top with additional ice to fill the glass
  - Grate fresh nutmeg over the top
  - Add garnishes and ignite the cinnamon stick so it is smoldering
  -  Serve with a small straw
